`/goal` and `/plan` (`handleStartWorkflow` in `src/commands/register-commands.ts`) generate `displayName` before `startNewWorkItem` allocates `.pi/realgoal/draft/<safeDirName>` by calling `ctx.modelRegistry.streamSimple(ctx.model, ...)` (8s timeout, `maxRetries: 0`, `cacheRetention: "none"`).

- Models with `model.reasoning && model.thinkingLevelMap?.off === null` (mandatory-reasoning models like `gpt-5` or `gemini-2.5-pro`) pass `reasoning: "minimal", thinkingBudgets: { minimal: 128 }, maxTokens: 320`; all other models omit `reasoning` with `maxTokens: 64`.
- Output is sanitized with `cleanGeneratedTitle` (strips quotes/markdown/title prefixes/leading path chars and validates `sanitizeTaskStem`), falling back to `deriveShortDisplayName` on error, abort, or missing `streamSimple`.
